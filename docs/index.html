Privacy Policy for NORAcraft

Application: NORAcraft Video Editor (com.itamadersomajinc.noracraft)
Developer: Itamadersomaj Inc
Effective Date: October 30, 2026

🔒 Core Commitment: 100% Private & On-Device

NORAcraft is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. Your videos, photos, voice recordings, and edited projects never leave your phone. We do not track your activity, collect personal identities, or transmit your media to any cloud servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike cloud-reliant video editors, NORAcraft respects your absolute digital sovereignty:

  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need an email, phone number, or login to use the app.
  • No Media Uploads: Your video clips, images, sound effects, and voice recordings are processed purely in local RAM and storage on your physical device.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not embed behavioral tracking SDKs, ad networks, or device fingerprinting services.
  • No Location Data: We never request, access, or track your GPS or network location.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

NORAcraft only requests Android system permissions strictly necessary to perform offline video and audio editing functions:

  • Photos, Videos & Media (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to let you select photos and videos from your gallery to import into your project timeline.
  • Audio Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Enables you to pick local music tracks or sounds stored on your device for your background audio.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required only if you actively choose to record voice-overs or dubbing directly within the timeline. Audio is saved solely on your device.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Keeps video rendering active and displays an export progress bar in your notification tray if you switch apps while rendering 1080p/4K video.
  • Internet Permission (INTERNET): Used exclusively to download public, open-source AI model weights (such as offline multilingual speech recognition and voice models from verified repositories) when requested by the user. No media, personal data, or project content is ever uploaded.

3. On-Device AI Features (Captions & Speech Synthesis)

NORAcraft provides advanced automated captions and text-to-speech powered by on-device inference engines (such as Sherpa-ONNX / Whisper and Piper):

  • Speech recognition is performed entirely by your phone's processor (CPU/NPU).
  • Audio waveforms are decoded locally into text without streaming voice data to external servers.
  • Text-to-speech audio is generated on your hardware and never sent to cloud APIs.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

Because NORAcraft does not collect personal or sensitive user data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All project metadata, drafts, audio recordings, and exported videos are stored locally in your app's private sandboxed directory or in your device's public media folder as chosen by you.

  • You can delete any project, draft, or exported file at any time directly in the app or via your file manager.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all internal project files and cache stored within the app sandbox.

6. Children's Privacy

NORAcraft does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). As our software performs all functions offline and does not collect personal data, it complies with global children's privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR-K.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
